The EXO method

Trust is part
of the model.

  1. 01

    Freeze the machine

    Market, attachment, undercarriage, operator station, and exact configuration are named before release geometry.

  2. 02

    Bind every claim

    Manufacturer facts, observed form, derived values, reconstructions, and unresolved gaps remain distinct.

  3. 03

    Prove the artifact

    Deterministic Blender builds, exact hashes, semantic hierarchy, geometry gates, and direct visual review travel together.

  4. 04

    Publish honestly

    A structural study is not a solver, collision proof, digital twin, training tool, or manufacturer endorsement.
