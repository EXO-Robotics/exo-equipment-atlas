# Initial roadmap

## Gate 0 — completed locally

- Neutral repository initialized.
- Evidence and rights policies established.
- Three official specification PDFs acquired into a gitignored research area.
- Candidate configuration, mechanism, source, and fact contracts created.
- Tracked-contract and private-source hash validation established.

## Gate 1 — configuration freeze

Prioritize the Deere 333 P-Tier. Resolve bucket family, attachment interface,
track width, cab/door state, grade-control package, market, and visual livery.
Then bind every admitted dimension to that exact selection.

For the Cat 320, resolve the 4.2-ton counterweight, Reach 5.7 m boom, R2.9B1
stick, 1.19 m3 HD bucket, 790 mm triple-grouser shoes, long undercarriage,
coupler/thumb state, cab guard, and market package.

For the Deere 310 P-Tier, resolve engine market, cab/canopy, MFWD, standard or
extendable dipperstick, front bucket, rear bucket, couplers, and counterweight.

## Gate 2 — reference boards and structural studies

Acquire first-party orthographic-like views, mechanism poses, attachment views,
and service/parts evidence where publicly and lawfully available. Record hidden
pivots and anchors as unresolved until applicable evidence exists.

## Gate 3 — first machine production

Build the independently authored 333 P-Tier hierarchy and structural blockout,
then implement its lift-path and bucket-tilt solver. Do not begin visual polish
until envelope, pivot continuity, cylinder continuity, ground clearance, and
collision proxies pass.
