# Rights and publication boundary

The research workflow may use lawfully accessible first-party publications to
understand visible equipment and published specifications. Those publications
remain private inputs and are not redistributed here.

Public prototypes must use independently authored geometry, neutral materials,
no manufacturer logos, and a clear unofficial/not-endorsed notice unless written
authorization says otherwise. Manufacturer colors, distinctive livery, trade
dress, photography, CAD, manuals, and marketing artwork are not assumed safe for
commercial use merely because they are publicly viewable.

Before an exact branded commercial release, obtain a project-specific legal
review and any necessary written permissions. This document records project
policy; it is not legal advice.
